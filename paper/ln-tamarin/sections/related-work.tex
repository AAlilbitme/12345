\section{Related Work}

\itparagraph{Formal verification of payment channels}
Litos \etal~\cite{litos2026formally} give a formally verified Lightning
implementation in \mwhy, proving functional correctness of a single channel.
Their treatment is deeper than ours at the channel layer and absent at the
routing layer: there is no multi-hop path, no fee arithmetic, and no
adversarial network model. Symbolic analyses in \mtamarin\ have targeted
two-party HTLC constructions such as cross-chain swaps and contingent payments;
these lack multi-hop routing, lifecycle revocation, and staggered CLTV
arithmetic.

\itparagraph{Model checking}
Grundmann and Hartenstein~\cite{grundmann2026security} verify balance security
for Lightning channels with TLA$^+$, using a stepwise refinement. Model
checking buys them a faithful treatment of concurrency that a symbolic prover
does not naturally provide; the cost is that their analysis is bounded --- in
the number of sessions, the depth of the state space, and the path length. Our
analysis is unbounded in sessions and in adversary derivation, and, at the
abstract layer, in path length. The two approaches are complementary rather
than competing.

\itparagraph{Agent skipping and path integrity}
Generic symbolic frameworks have studied path integrity and agent-skipping in
message-forwarding protocols~\cite{smith2022modelling}. That line of work
establishes that an intermediary can be bypassed, and does so at a level of
generality that covers Lightning as an instance. Their formalism, however,
carries no numeric sort: it can express \emph{that} an agent was skipped but
not \emph{what} the skip cost. Our contribution at this point is not the
attack, which is known~\cite{malavolta2019anonymous}, but its quantification
(\Cref{thm:wormhole}).

\itparagraph{Attacks on Lightning}
Malavolta \etal~\cite{malavolta2019anonymous} introduced the wormhole attack
and proposed anonymous multi-hop locks as a countermeasure. Tikhomirov
\etal~\cite{tikhomirov2020quantitative} give a quantitative analysis of
security, anonymity and scalability. Aumayr
\etal~\cite{aumayr2021generalized} construct generalised channels from limited
scripts. We reproduce the wormhole as a fidelity check on our model rather than
as a new finding.
