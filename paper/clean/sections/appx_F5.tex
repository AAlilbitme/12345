\subsection{PTLC Model: Lock Set-up and Hop Settlement}\label{lst:ptlc}

These are the two rules of the PTLC model (\code{lightning_aux.spthy}, \code{-D=PTLC}) that
implement the per-hop locks of Sec.~\ref{sec:ptlc}. \code{Sender_Setup} draws the fresh secret
$x$, the channels \texttt{c0} to \texttt{c3} and the tweaks $t_1, t_2, t_3$, derives the channel
secrets by the hash chain, locks the sender's channel with $h(s_0)$, gives each hop its incoming
channel, outgoing channel, outgoing lock and tweak, and publishes all four locks.
\begin{spthy}
rule Sender_Setup:
    let s3 = ~x
        s2 = h(<s3, ~t3>)
        s1 = h(<s2, ~t2>)
        s0 = h(<s1, ~t1>)
    in
    [ Fr(~x), Fr(~c0), Fr(~c1), Fr(~c2), Fr(~c3), Fr(~t1), Fr(~t2), Fr(~t3) ]
    --[ Pay($S, $H1, $H2, $H3, $R, ~c0, ~c1, ~c2, ~c3),
        Neq($S, $H1), Neq($S, $H2), Neq($S, $H3), Neq($S, $R),
        Neq($H1, $H2), Neq($H2, $H3), Neq($H1, $H3),
        Neq($H1, $R), Neq($H2, $R), Neq($H3, $R) ]->
    [ !Lock(~c0, $S, $H1, h(s0)), Open(~c0), SenderWait($S, ~c0),
      !Hop($H1, ~c0, ~c1, $H2, h(s1), ~t1),
      !Hop($H2, ~c1, ~c2, $H3, h(s2), ~t2),
      !Hop($H3, ~c2, ~c3, $R,  h(s3), ~t3),
      !RecvSecret($R, ~c3, ~x),
      Out(h(s0)), Out(h(s1)), Out(h(s2)), Out(h(s3)) ]
\end{spthy}
\code{Hop_Settle} lets an honest hop that learns the secret \texttt{sec} of its outgoing channel
claim its incoming channel with the secret derived from \texttt{sec} and its own tweak, and
publishes that secret.
\begin{spthy}
rule Hop_Settle:
    [ Pend($H, cIn, cOut, t), ClaimInfo(cOut, sec), !Lock(cIn, P, $H, Lin), Open(cIn) ]
    --[ Redeemed(cIn), HopSettled($H, cIn, cOut), Eq(h(h(<sec, t>)), Lin) ]->
    [ ClaimInfo(cIn, h(<sec, t>)), Out(h(<sec, t>)) ]
\end{spthy}
In the HTLC baseline (\code{-D=HTLC}), \code{Sender_Setup} binds \texttt{s0} to \texttt{s3} all
to \texttt{\textasciitilde x}, and \code{Hop_Settle} passes the outgoing secret on unchanged:
\begin{spthy}
rule Hop_Settle:
    [ Pend($H, cIn, cOut, t), ClaimInfo(cOut, sec), !Lock(cIn, P, $H, Lin), Open(cIn) ]
    --[ Redeemed(cIn), HopSettled($H, cIn, cOut), Eq(h(sec), Lin) ]->
    [ ClaimInfo(cIn, sec), Out(sec) ]
\end{spthy}
