Review notes (no effect on the verified safety results, but state them in the paper):
- Fees are not linked across hops: each Forward_HTLC reads %vIn/%fee/%vOut from the network,
  so no claim about amounts or fee theft follows from this model.
- Free(ptr) recycling is inconsistent (Intermediary_Settle frees ptrIn only; Receiver_Fulfill_HTLC
  frees nothing). Only limits channel reuse.
- Receiver_Fulfill_HTLC does not check that the route belongs to the invoice's sender (harmless:
  Eq(h(x), y) binds the preimage).
- Fulfill_Compromised_In can create a Fulfill on a channel with no pending HTLC (dead fact).
- Never_Loses / Payment_Atomicity assume "never compromised"; T2/T3 use "not compromised before
  #k". The lemmas are correct but weaker than they could be.
